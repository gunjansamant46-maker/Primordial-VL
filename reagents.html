🧪 Reagents & Stains

Methylene Blue

Purpose: Stains nucleus in cells

Principle: Binds to negatively charged DNA

Result: Nucleus appears blue

Precaution: Avoid over-staining

Crystal Violet

Purpose: Primary stain in Gram staining

Principle: Retained in thick cell wall

Result: Gram-positive bacteria appear purple

Precaution: Wash properly

Litmus Paper

Purpose: To identify whether a substance is acidic or basic.

Principle:

  • Acid → blue litmus turns red
  • Base → red litmus turns blue

Result:

  • Red = acidic
  • Blue = basic

Precaution:

  • Use clean droppers/glass rods
  • Avoid contamination
  • Do not reuse contaminated litmus
  • Observe color immediately

Universal pH Indicator

Purpose: To determine approximate pH of a solution.

Principle: Contains multiple dyes that show different colors at different pH levels.

Result:

  • Red/Orange → acidic
  • Green → neutral
  • Blue/Purple → basic

Precaution:

  • Use small quantities
  • Use clean apparatus
  • Compare quickly with pH chart
  • Avoid accidental mixing

Safranin

Purpose: Counterstain

Principle: Stains cells that lose primary stain

Result: Gram-negative bacteria appear pink/red

Precaution: Avoid excess staining

Hydrochloric Acid (HCl)

Purpose: Acidification

Principle: Releases H⁺ ions

Result: Low pH

Precaution: Corrosive chemical

Sodium Hydroxide (NaOH)

Purpose: Base formation

Principle: Releases OH⁻ ions

Result: High pH

Precaution: Can cause burns
